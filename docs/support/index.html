FAQ

How do I create a tracker?

Add a new tracker from the Home screen, give it a name, a color and an icon, then tap play to start timing.

Can I edit or add time I forgot to track?

Yes. Open a tracker to edit existing entries or add new ones at any time.

How do I delete a tracker?

Swipe on the tracker in the Home screen list and tap delete.

How do goals work?

Each tracker can have a daily, weekly, monthly or yearly goal. Segment bars fill in as you log time so you can see how close you are.

How do I see my timer on the Lock Screen?

When a timer is running, Chopped shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on supported iPhones. Make sure Live Activities are enabled for Chopped in Settings.

Are there widgets and Control Center controls?

Yes. Add a Chopped widget to your Home Screen to start and stop timers without opening the app. Control Center controls require iOS 18 or later.

Is my data backed up or synced?

No. Chopped stores everything only on your iPhone and makes no network requests. Deleting the app deletes your data.

What does it cost?

Chopped is free, with no ads and no account required. It needs iOS 17 or later.
