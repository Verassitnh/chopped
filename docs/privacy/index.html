Chopped: Time Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Chopped does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data storage

Your activities and time sessions are stored only on your device. The app never sends this data to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the data.

No tracking, ads, or accounts

Chopped has no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no account or sign-in. It makes no network requests.

Children

Because no data is collected, Chopped is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
